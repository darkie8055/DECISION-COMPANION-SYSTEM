# 📋 Decision Companion - Quick Reference Card

## 🎯 The 5-Step Process

### 1️⃣ **Choose Your Starting Point**
- Click a template (pre-built decision type)
- Or create custom decision
- **Time:** 30 seconds

### 2️⃣ **Define What Matters**
- List 3-7 criteria (factors that matter)
- Give each a weight (%) showing importance
- Example: Price 40%, Quality 60%
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes

### 3️⃣ **Add Your Options**
- Enter each choice you're comparing
- Example: Option A, Option B, Option C
- Can add/remove anytime
- **Time:** 1 minute

### 4️⃣ **Score Each Option**
- Rate each option on each criterion (0-10)
- 0-3 = poor, 4-6 = okay, 7-10 = excellent
- Be honest, not aspirational
- **Time:** 3-5 minutes

### 5️⃣ **Review Results**
- See weighted scores
- Check risk assessment
- Test sensitivity analysis
- Save for later
- **Time:** 2-3 minutes

**Total Time:** 10-15 minutes

## 📊 Scoring Scale Explained

| Score | Meaning | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| 0 | Doesn't meet criterion at all | Laptop: No battery life |
| 1-3 | Poorly meets criterion | Laptop: 2 hour battery |
| 4-5 | Somewhat meets criterion | Laptop: 6 hour battery |
| 6 | Adequately meets criterion | Laptop: 8 hour battery |
| 7-8 | Well meets criterion | Laptop: 12 hour battery |
| 9 | Excellently meets criterion | Laptop: 15+ hour battery |
| 10 | Perfectly meets criterion | Laptop: All-day battery |

## 💡 Pro Tips

### ✅ Scoring Tips
- Be objective (facts, not feelings)
- Use full 0-10 scale
- Be consistent within criteria
- Don't assume - know your facts

### ⚖️ Weight Tips
- Higher % = more important to you
- All weights must = 100%
- First weights don't have to be perfect
- Test different weights to see impact

### 📊 Results Tips
1. **Analysis Tab:** See overall weighted scores
2. **Risk Tab:** Find potential problems
3. **Sensitivity Tab:** Test "what-if" scenarios
4. **Comparison Tab:** See side-by-side view

### 🔄 Sensitivity Testing
- What if I change priorities?
- How much does salary matter?
- Would the decision flip?
- What trade-offs am I making?

### 💾 Saving Tips
- Save important decisions
- Return to refine scores
- Track decision history
- Learn from past choices

## ❓ Quick Answers

**Q: How many criteria should I use?**
A: 3-7 is ideal. More gets overwhelming.

**Q: Can I change my scores?**
A: Yes! Load saved decisions anytime.

**Q: What if all options score the same?**
A: They're similar. Check sensitivity or reconsider criteria.

**Q: Should I use templates or custom?**
A: Templates = faster. Custom = full control.

**Q: How do weights work?**
A: They multiply your scores. Higher % = more impact.

**Q: Can I share my decision?**
A: Yes! Use the Share button in results.

**Q: Where are my saved decisions?**
A: "My Recent Decisions" section on home screen.

**Q: Can I delete a decision?**
A: Yes, but you can't undo it.

**Q: How do I know if I'm doing this right?**
A: No "right" way! Your honest scores are correct.

## 📊 Example: Job Decision

### Step 1: Define Criteria
```
1. Salary
2. Work Culture
3. Growth Opportunities
4. Work-Life Balance
5. Location
```

### Step 2: Set Weights
```
Salary:              40%  (critical for me)
Culture:             30%  (very important)
Growth:              15%  (somewhat important)
Work-Life Balance:   10%  (nice to have)
Location:             5%  (flexible)
Total:              100%
```

### Step 3: Add Options
```
- Company A
- Company B
- Company C
- Company D
```

### Step 4: Score Each
```
         Salary Culture Growth Work-Life Location
Company A:  9      8      7       6        8
Company B:  7      9      8       8        7
Company C:  8      7      9       9        5
Company D:  6      9      9       9        9
```

### Step 5: Results
```
Company A: 8.1/10 (Best for salary, solid all-around)
Company B: 8.0/10 (Great culture, good balance)
Company C: 7.9/10 (Best growth/balance, low location)
Company D: 7.6/10 (Best balance, lower salary)
```

**Decision:** Company A seems best for your priorities.
**Next:** Check risk tab for potential problems.
